Hi Alex,

This essay asked you to evaluate the relative importance of the long-term and short-term causes of the First World War, using at least three scholarly sources. A strong response weighs the causes against each other rather than simply listing them.

Overall this is a solid piece of work and you should be proud of it. Your introduction sets out a clear thesis: that the alliance system turned a regional crisis into a world war. Your discussion of militarism in the second body paragraph is well researched and makes good use of Clark's The Sleepwalkers.

However, the essay tends to describe each cause in turn without comparing them, so the evaluation the question asks for is mostly missing. In paragraph four, you state that Franz Ferdinand was assassinated on 28 July 1914; it was 28 June. Some of your in-text citations are also missing page numbers.

For your next essay, try ending each body paragraph with a sentence that links the cause back to your thesis and says how important it was compared with the others. Drawing up a quick table of the causes and ranking them before you start writing may help you plan this. Before you submit, reread the question and ask yourself whether every paragraph answers it.

Well done, and keep it up!
