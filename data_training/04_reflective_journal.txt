Feedback on Reflective Journal, Week 6

For this journal you were asked to reflect on a critical incident from your clinical placement using Gibbs' reflective cycle, moving from description through to an action plan.

Thank you for sharing such an honest reflection; you are clearly a caring and thoughtful student nurse. Your description of the medication round is detailed and easy to follow, and you are open about how you felt at the time.

The analysis stage is the weakest part. You describe what happened, but you don't link the incident to the literature on patient safety, which the rubric requires. In the third paragraph you say the interruption "caused" the near miss, but you don't explain why interruptions increase the risk of error.

Your action plan is vague ("be more careful next time"). A stronger plan would name a specific strategy, such as wearing a "do not disturb" vest during medication rounds, and say how you will know whether it has worked.

I notice you are starting to question your own practice rather than just report it, which is exactly what reflective writing aims for. Keep asking yourself "why did I act that way?" as you write the next entries, and check each one against the stages of the cycle before you submit.
